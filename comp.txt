# Complexity Analysis

## Adjacency Matrix

Space Complexity: O(V²)

BFS Time Complexity: O(V²)

DFS Time Complexity: O(V²)

Edge Checking: O(1)

Vertex Search: O(V)


## Adjacency List

Space Complexity: O(V + E)

BFS Time Complexity: O(V + E)

DFS Time Complexity: O(V + E)

Edge Checking: O(degree)

Vertex Search: O(V)


For the given graph:

V = 6
E = 6
